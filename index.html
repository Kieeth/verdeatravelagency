Walkability & Tour Evaluation

Help us assess Baguio's streetscapes for slow tourism using the Hanibuchi Walkability Checklist.

Infrastructure

1. Is there a sidewalk along this street segment?

2. Is the sidewalk wide enough for comfortable pedestrian use?

3. Are there obstructions blocking the pedestrian path (e.g., parked motorcycles, merchandise, debris)?

4. Are there steep slopes that make walking difficult or uncomfortable?

Traffic & Safety

5. Is there street parking that encroaches on or conflicts with pedestrian space?

6. Is there heavy vehicular traffic that makes walking feel unsafe or unpleasant?

7. Is there sufficient foot traffic to indicate the street is actively used by pedestrians?

8. Is there a marked crosswalk for safe road crossing?

9. Are traffic mirrors present at intersections or blind spots?

Amenities & Aesthetics

10. Are streetlights present and functional along this segment?

11. Are street trees or other greenery present along the sidewalk?

12. Does the streetscape have attractive visual elements (e.g., facades, landscaping, heritage features)?

13. Is there visible graffiti or litter that detracts from the environment?

14. Are there abandoned or derelict buildings visible along this segment?
